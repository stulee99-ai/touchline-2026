- Coppa Italia: fixed bracket (real). Preliminary 9 Aug, R1 14–17 Aug, R2 1–2 Sep, R16 2 & 16 Dec (seeds 1–8 at home: Inter, Napoli, Roma, Como, Milan, Juventus, Atalanta, Bologna), QF 3 & 10 Feb, SF 3 Mar & 21 Apr (two legs), final Wed 19 May 2027 (Olimpico).
