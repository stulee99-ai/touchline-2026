## How to read this file
- Fixture lines use this format: `YYYY-MM-DD | HOME | AWAY | competition | note(optional)`
  - The optional 5th field holds a result (`FT h-a`, from the home side's view), the venue, or a flag such as `DATE NOT PUBLISHED`.
  - The date is the local match date. CONCACAF dates are as concacaf.com lists them (US Eastern time).
  - Codes are FIFA trigrams. Teams outside your list also use FIFA codes: FRO, KAZ, MDA, LVA, BLR, SMR, AND, GIB, MLT, AZE, LIE, CUW, TRI, NCA, GUA, SLV, GUY, DMA, CAY, BER, LCA, BRB, LES, MWI, SSD, SOM, RSA, ERI, ZIM, NAM, LBY, BOT, UGA, SDN, ETH, RWA, LBR, MAD, IRN, PLE, IND, SYR, MDV, SIN, BAN, VIE, PAK, TJK, STP, IRQ, OMA, KUW.
  - `TBD`, `WINNER(...)` and `LOSER(...)` mark knockout slots that are not decided yet.
- **Russia (RUS)** has been suspended from all FIFA and UEFA competitions since 28 Feb 2022. It is not in the Nations League, and its Euro 2028 participation is "TBC", pending any lifting of the ban. It plays friendlies only; those are listed.
- **Guadeloupe (GLP) and Martinique (MTQ)** are CONCACAF members but not FIFA members. Both play in the CONCACAF Nations League: MTQ in League A Group 2 and GLP in League B Group B.
